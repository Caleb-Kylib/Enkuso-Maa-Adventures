MEET DAN NAKOLA

Guided by home.

Enkuso Maa Adventures was founded by Dan Nakola, a nature and cultural guide born and raised in the Maasai Mara. His connection to this place is personal: the rhythms of the land, the stories of its people, and the responsibility to welcome guests with care.

“I want every visitor to leave with more than photographs. I want them to feel the generosity, knowledge, and dignity of my community.”
